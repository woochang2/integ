\begin{block}{1. Motivation: The Cut Rule Problem}
\large
Modern DAG-based BFT protocols decouple data dissemination from ordering. Validators broadcast batches into a DAG; a consensus leader periodically \textbf{cuts} the DAG to commit transactions.

\vspace{0.5cm}
\textbf{Key Question:} How should the leader decide \emph{which} data to include in each commit?

\vspace{0.5cm}
This choice---the \textbf{cut rule}---determines:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2cm]
    \item[\textbullet] \textbf{Straggler resilience:} Do slow validators delay commits?
    \item[\textbullet] \textbf{Transaction fairness:} Are all validators' data eventually included?
    \item[\textbullet] \textbf{Hangover effect:} How fast does the system recover after blips?
\end{itemize}
\end{block}

\vspace{1cm}

\begin{block}{3. Key Insight}
\large
Both systems share the \textbf{same broadcast quorum} ($f{+}1$) for speculative data availability, but diverge in how the leader constructs commit proposals:

\vspace{0.5cm}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lcc@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Property} & \textbf{Relaxed Mempool} & \textbf{Autobahn} \\
\midrule
Cut scope        & Top $2f{+}1$       & All $n$ lanes \\
Slow node policy & Exclude            & Include at own tip \\
Commit size      & $O(H_{\min} \cdot n)$ & $O(n)$ tip refs \\
Hangover         & Gradual recovery   & Instant recovery \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\end{block}

\begin{alertblock}{5. Conclusion \& Future Work}
\large

\vspace{0.5cm}

\textbf{Neither cut strategy dominates universally:}

\vspace{0.3cm}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2cm]
    \item[\color{red!70!black}\textbullet] \textbf{Conservative cut:} Best for persistent stragglers (lower latency)
    \item[\color{orange!70!black}\textbullet] \textbf{Inclusive cut:} Best for blip-prone environments (seamless recovery)
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.5cm}

\textbf{Future Direction:} \emph{Adaptive cut} that monitors validator height disparity and dynamically switches between conservative and inclusive modes. Both strategies share the same DAG substrate, making runtime switching feasible.

\vspace{0.5cm}
\end{alertblock}
